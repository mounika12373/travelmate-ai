# Geographical Expansion Plan

This document outlines the plans for expanding the geographical scope and user reach of **TravelMate AI**.

## 1. Short-Term: Regional Hubs (Weeks 1-4)
* **Goal**: Focus on localizing regional tourism in India.
* **Target Destinations**: Expand travel guide datasets for southern and northern Indian hubs (e.g., Delhi, Bangalore, Visakhapatnam, Mumbai).
* **Outreach**: Team up with local campus clubs to distribute offline-ready travel pamphlets with QR codes linking to the app.

## 2. Medium-Term: Multilingual Indic Outreach (Months 2-3)
* **Goal**: Enable travel guides for non-English speakers.
* **Activities**:
  - Scale up localized Indic language translation support (Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Bengali).
  - Highlight localized safety guidelines and travel restrictions specific to different states.

## 3. Long-Term: Global Travel & Cultural Integration (Months 4-6)
* **Goal**: Target international travelers visiting India and vice versa.
* **Activities**:
  - Connect with travel agencies and tourism boards.
  - Expand emergency services databases globally.
  - Optimize the app to load quickly on low-end mobile networks in remote geographical locations.
