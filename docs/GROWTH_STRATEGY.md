# User Base Growth Strategy & Outreach Plan

This document outlines our strategy to expand the user base of **TravelMate AI** over a 4-week cycle.

## Week-wise Outreach Plan

### Week 1: Soft Launch & Local Validation
* **Target**: Campus students and project mentors.
* **Activities**: 
  - Share the local and staging instances with peers to test usability.
  - Collect initial feedback on UI aesthetics and itinerary helper sheets.
  - Fix high-priority bugs reported during initial usage.

### Week 2: Social Media & Portfolio Marketing
* **Target**: Tech enthusiasts, travel bloggers, and open-source developers.
* **Activities**:
  - Publish the project on LinkedIn highlighting the modern Outfit-font dashboard, glassmorphism design, and multi-lingual chatbot.
  - Write a medium-length tech article on building interactive Plotly charts in Streamlit for travel budget planning.

### Week 3: Target Traveler Groups & Communities
* **Target**: Backpackers, students planning study trips, and weekend travelers.
* **Activities**:
  - Promote the app in online student travel groups and forums.
  - Provide a walkthrough tutorial on using the multi-lingual assistant for checking local laws and safety rules.

### Week 4: Feature Refinement & Indicator Integration
* **Target**: Broad non-technical audience.
* **Activities**:
  - Polish Indic language support (Telugu and Hindi) to make the UI and chatbot highly accessible in rural/regional sectors.
  - Collect user engagement metrics to analyze popular queries and destinations.
